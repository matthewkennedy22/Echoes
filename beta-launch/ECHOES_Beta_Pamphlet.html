ECHOES

Local history, made conversational.

ECHOES lets visitors converse with figures from California history, grounded in public-domain sources, primary records, and curated claim banks. Answers are labeled by evidence. Citations are one tap away. When the record is incomplete, the figure says so.

Not a generic chatbot. History that stays accountable to the evidence.

Try the live beta

https://echoes-inky-zeta.vercel.app/

Any phone or computer. No app. No account.

Note: First question for a figure may take 30–60 seconds while sources load; then it is much faster.

Mission

  • Make local and regional history more accessible and engaging
  • Keep AI answers tied to real sources, with visible evidence
  • Partner with museums, archives, educators, and societies, not replace them
  • Grow from web prototype to living portrait exhibit experiences communities can host

Long term: walk up to a portrait that comes to life. Conversation that sends visitors back into cases, maps, and photographs.

How it works

  • Ask in plain language or tap a starter
  • Evidence labels: Documented · Reasonable inference · Contested · Not in the sources
  • Show evidence for citations · Hear this for period voice
  • Historic images when the topic fits
  • Honest refusal when sources do not support an answer

# ECHOES Beta Partnership Outline

A plain-language summary of expectations during the beta. This sets clear terms; it is not a heavy legal contract.

---

## The short version

**Your organization** agrees to try ECHOES and share feedback. **ECHOES (Matthew Kennedy)** provides free beta access (web link / QR), optional collaborative custom-figure work, and all technical setup. Either side may end the beta at any time.

---

## What ECHOES provides

- **Free beta access** to California Speaks (seven source-grounded figures) via web link and/or QR code  
- **Live demo:** https://echoes-inky-zeta.vercel.app/  
- No installation or IT work required  
- All technical work, maintenance, and updates  
- **Evidence-labeled, source-grounded** answers with visible citations  
- Use of **only public-domain, partner-owned, or permissioned** source material  
- **Partner approval** of any content drawn from your collection before it is used  
- Optional: a **custom figure for your town, region, or exhibit** as a collaborative pilot (sources researched together; you help define scope and approve materials)  
- For high-volume public use: transparency on AI usage and an optional **monthly spending cap**

## What the partner provides

- Willingness to **use ECHOES and give honest feedback** (accuracy, gaps, usefulness for visitors)  
- Optional: **suggestions** of sources, photographs, topics, or a custom-figure candidate  
- Optional: a **short testimonial or case-study quote** if the experience is positive  
- For high-volume public deployments (if agreed): **at-cost AI usage** within a pre-set monthly cap

## Costs

- **No licensing or subscription fee during the beta**  
- Light organizational trials: free to run  
- High-volume exhibit / public use: partner may cover **AI usage at cost** (typically a few dollars a month); **monthly cap** available; no markup during beta

## Content, rights & accuracy

- ECHOES uses only **public-domain, partner-owned, or explicitly permissioned** material  
- The partner **retains all rights** to its own materials; nothing here transfers ownership  
- ECHOES is an **AI simulation** and may contain errors; the partner is **not responsible** for chatbot outputs; ECHOES will act on accuracy feedback promptly

## Privacy

- The public uses ECHOES **without creating accounts**  
- Data collection is kept minimal; retention can be tailored to the partner

## Term & exit

- The beta is **month-to-month and informal**  
- Either party may pause or end participation **at any time, for any reason,** with no penalty

## The roadmap (what the beta builds toward)

- **Now:** text + optional voice + matched historic images (California Speaks + optional custom pilots)  
- **Next:** classroom / gallery deployments, richer regional coverage  
- **Goal:** a **living portrait**—a physical exhibit experience that sends visitors back into the collections. Beta partners help shape this direction.

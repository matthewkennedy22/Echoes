## The offer (memorize this)

> Free beta access to source-grounded California history conversations—and, if useful, a collaborative custom figure for your town, region, or exhibit. No app, no IT project. Evidence labels + citations. When the record is incomplete, the figure says so.

## This week’s playbook

1. Export pamphlet PDF from `beta-launch/ECHOES_Beta_Pamphlet.html`  
2. Open `Lead_List.csv` in Sheets/Excel  
3. Send **Email A** from `Outreach_Emails.md` to Priority **A** leads (8–12/day)  
4. Personalize one hook line per org  
5. Follow up after 7–10 days  
6. Book 15-minute demos with anyone who replies  

**Do not** BCC mass lists. Verify “Needs lookup” emails on the org site before sending.
